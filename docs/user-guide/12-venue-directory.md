# Venue Directory

Search halls, hotels and resorts and start a proposal at one. Today the directory covers Thiruvananthapuram and around (29 venues); directories for more cities are on the roadmap.

## Finding a venue
- Search by name (also-known-as names work too).
- Filter by **type** (hotels, resorts, event venues) and **area**.
- The featured venue is shown first as a large card.

## A venue's details
Open a venue for its event spaces and capacities, star rating, address, phone, website, a link to its own photo gallery, and where the facts came from.

## Starting a proposal
**Start proposal** begins a quotation with the venue already filled in.

## Tips
- The Venue field in a proposal suggests directory venues, and warns when the guests are more than the venue's largest space holds.
- Venue facts were researched from official sites and listings. **Confirm capacities and details with the venue before quoting them.**
- Venues without a licensed photo show a monogram instead.
