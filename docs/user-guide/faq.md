# Frequently asked questions

## Data and devices
**Where is my work saved?** In the browser on the device you use. Expenses and screenshots too. Cloud sync is on the roadmap.

**How do I move my work to another device?** Proposal Studio → **Back up all**, then **Import** on the other device. The CRM, Tasks, Vendors and Questionnaires each have their own Export and Import. See [Getting started](01-getting-started.md#backing-up-and-moving-to-another-device).

**I cleared my browser and my proposals are gone.** Import your latest backup. Without one, the data can't be recovered — back up weekly.

**Can two people work on the same proposal?** Not at the same time today. One person owns it on their device and shares a backup file.

## Money
**Why isn't an additional option in the Grand Total?** Additional options, upgrades marked *Offer* and as-per-actuals items are listed separately for the family to choose. They're never added. See the [Glossary](glossary.md).

**Is GST included in the Grand Total?** No. The Grand Total is before GST; GST and the total including GST are reference figures. Payment milestones are shares of the total including GST.

**Can a client ever see our costs or margin?** No. They're removed from every PDF, Excel file, web page and portal.

## Sharing
**How should I send a private link?** Send the link and password separately — for example the link by email and the password by WhatsApp.

**A client says the link doesn't work.** Links end after 7, 30 or 90 days, or when revoked, and a wrong password looks the same. Create a new link, or **Update portal** for a client portal.

**Does the WhatsApp button send the message?** No. It opens WhatsApp with the message ready; nothing goes until you press Send.

**Can clients take screenshots?** Event Bridge watermarks pages and blanks them on common screenshot shortcuts, but no website can fully stop screenshots or a phone camera.

## Sign-in
**I was signed out while sharing.** Sessions last 12 hours. Choose **Sign in again**; the open proposal reopens afterwards.

**I forgot my password.** Ask your admin to reset it. See [Admin setup](admin-setup.md#team-accounts).

**My phone keyboard adds a space or capital letter.** Stray spaces and invisible characters are ignored, and the password box turns off auto-capitalise. Passwords are still case-sensitive.
