# Venue layout & seating

Draw each function's floor plan, seat the guests at its tables, and print the seating plan for the venue team and the welcome desk.

## The floor plan (Layout tab)
Open **Guests & Seating** from the Dashboard and press **Layout** on a wedding (or open the wedding from **Events**, then **Layout**). With several functions, pick one at the top; **Copy the … plan** starts from another function's plan.

### Starting
- **Reception rounds**: stage, dance floor, buffet, bar and round tables of 10.
- **Banquet long tables**: head table and long tables of 8.
- **Mandap with chair rows**: mandap at the front, an aisle, and rows of chairs.
- A **saved plan** for this venue, or an **empty room** (30 × 20 m to begin).

Starters are sized for your guests (the confirmed headcount, or the proposal's guest count).

### Editing
| To… | Do this |
|---|---|
| Add something | Click it under **Add** (it appears in the middle of the view), or **drag it onto the plan** where you want it. Right-click the floor for *Add a round/long table here* |
| Move | Drag it. It snaps to 25 cm and **lines up with its neighbours** (gold guide lines show when edges or centres match); hold Alt to place it freely. Or use the arrow keys (Shift for 1 m steps) |
| Resize | Pull any of the **eight handles**. Round tables stay round. The size and seats show next to the pointer, and **the chairs follow the table's size** (a smaller table loses chairs) |
| Rotate | Drag the knob above the table (snaps every 15°), press R, or ⟳ on the toolbar for 90° |
| Change seats | **−** and **+** on the toolbar above the selected table, the + and − keys, or the Seats box in the panel |
| Lock in place | 🔒 on the toolbar or *Lock in place* in the right-click menu: a locked item can't be moved, resized or rotated |
| Copy, paste, duplicate | Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V (or right-click → *Paste here*), Ctrl+D, ⧉ on the toolbar |
| Front / back | Right-click → *Bring to front* / *Send to back* |
| Select several | Shift-click, or Shift-drag a box on the floor; then move them together, align, line up, space evenly or duplicate |
| Delete | Delete key, 🗑 on the toolbar, or right-click → *Delete* |
| Undo / redo | Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y, or the arrows in the toolbar |
| Pan and zoom | Drag the empty floor, scroll or pinch, or use − + and **Fit** |

On a phone, press and hold an item for the menu; the handles are bigger for fingers.

With nothing selected, the **Room** panel sets the room's size and compares the seats in the plan with the guests coming and the venue's capacity. Items that overlap are outlined in red.

**Save as a venue plan** keeps the room for the next wedding at the same venue. **Start again** clears the plan (and its seating).

## Seating (Seating tab)
1. The list on the left shows the households still to seat for this function: those who said yes, with how many. Tick *Include guests who haven't replied* to seat ahead of replies.
2. **Drag** a household onto a table, or onto a particular seat; the table lights up as you pass over it. On a phone, **tap** the household, then tap a table. Hover a seat to see who sits there.
3. **Auto-seat** fills empty seats with everyone left, keeping households together and groups (Family, College…) at the same tables where it can.
4. Click a table to see who sits there, unseat someone or clear the table.
5. Seats are coloured by side: bride's, groom's or both.

**Needs attention** lists anyone seated who has since said no, households split over tables, and people who lost their seat when a table was made smaller or removed. **Remove people not coming** tidies the first kind in one go.

## The seating PDF
**Download seating PDF** makes an A4 document with:
1. the floor plan, with taken seats filled in
2. each table and who sits at it
3. a *Find your table* list, A–Z, for the welcome desk
