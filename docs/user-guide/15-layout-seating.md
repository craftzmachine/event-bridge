# Venue layout & seating

Draw each function's floor plan, seat the guests at its tables, and print the seating plan for the venue team and the welcome desk.

## The floor plan (Layout tab)
Open **Guests & Seating** from the Dashboard and press **Layout** on a wedding (or open the wedding from **Events**, then **Layout**). With several functions, pick one at the top; **Copy the … plan** starts from another function's plan.

### Starting
- **Reception rounds**: stage, dance floor, buffet, bar and round tables of 10.
- **Banquet long tables**: head table and long tables of 8.
- **Mandap with chair rows**: mandap at the front, an aisle, and rows of chairs.
- A **saved plan** for this venue, or an **empty room** (30 × 20 m to begin).

Starters are sized for your guests (the confirmed headcount, or the proposal's guest count).

### Editing
| To… | Do this |
|---|---|
| Add something | Click it under **Add** (it appears in the middle of the view), or **drag it onto the plan** where you want it. Right-click the floor for *Add a round/long table here* |
| Move | Drag it. It snaps to 25 cm and **lines up with its neighbours** (gold guide lines show when edges or centres match); hold Alt to place it freely. Or use the arrow keys (Shift for 1 m steps) |
| Resize | Pull any of the **eight handles**. Round tables stay round. The size and seats show next to the pointer, and **the chairs follow the table's size** (a smaller table loses chairs) |
| Rotate | Drag the knob above the table (snaps every 15°), press R, or ⟳ on the toolbar for 90° |
| Change seats | **−** and **+** on the toolbar above the selected table, the + and − keys, or the Seats box in the panel. For many tables at once: select them and use **Seats each … Apply**, or **Tables ▾** to set every round (or long) table to the same number, or spread a number of guests across them |
| Exact sizes | Select an item: the panel shows its width and length (or diameter) in metres, and **Common sizes** such as mandap 4 × 4, 5 × 5, 6 × 6 or 8 × 8 m, stage 8 × 4 m, aisle 1.5 × 10 m, or round tables Ø1.5 m (8), Ø1.8 m (10), Ø2.1 m (12) |
| Lock in place | 🔒 on the toolbar or *Lock in place* in the right-click menu: a locked item can't be moved, resized or rotated |
| Copy, paste, duplicate | Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V (or right-click → *Paste here*), Ctrl+D, ⧉ on the toolbar |
| Front / back | Right-click → *Bring to front* / *Send to back* |
| Select several | Shift-click, or Shift-drag a box on the floor; then move them together, align, line up, space evenly or duplicate |
| Delete | Delete key, 🗑 on the toolbar, or right-click → *Delete* |
| Undo / redo | Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y, or the arrows in the toolbar |
| Pan and zoom | Drag the empty floor, scroll or pinch, or use − + and **Fit** |

On a phone, press and hold an item for the menu; the handles are bigger for fingers.

### What you can add
The **Add** panel is grouped (type in **Find an item** to filter):
- **Seating:** round tables, long tables, chair rows, sofas / lounge seating
- **Ceremony & stage:** mandap, stage, **aisle / runner**, LED wall / screen, dance floor, DJ / sound
- **Food & bar:** buffet counter, bar / juice counter
- **Décor & venue:** entrance, photo booth, flower stand, pillar, welcome desk, gifts table, kids' area, wall / divider, text label

The *Mandap with chair rows* starter now includes a centre aisle. An aisle is a floor covering, so it only counts as overlapping when a table or chair row stands on it.

### Counts and inventory
The **Counts** panel (right-hand side) totals tables by size and seats, chairs, chair rows and every other item with its size, as you work. **Inventory ▾** downloads the list for the rental vendor as PDF or Excel: chairs, tables by size, tablecloths to the floor, runners, centrepieces and décor with sizes.

### Tutorial
**🎓 Tutorial** (top of the Layout tab) walks you through the editor step by step on the plan you're in; each step ticks off when you do it. For a practice plan, use **Start the tutorial** in [Floor Plans](16-floor-plans.md).

### Seeing it clearly
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Realistic / Simple** | Realistic draws chairs with backrests, tablecloths with **numbered badges**, a chequered dance floor, the mandap's pillars and garland, the stage's skirt and steps, and soft shadows. Simple draws plain shapes. Your choice is remembered on this device |
| **Rulers** | Metre rulers along the top and left that follow as you pan and zoom |
| **📏 Measure** (or M) | Drag to measure any distance in metres; hold Shift to keep the line straight; Esc to finish |
| **Minimap** | The small map in the corner: the red box is what's on screen; click or drag on it to jump there |
| **▦ Grid of tables** | Add rows × columns of round tables, long tables or chair rows in one go, with the gap between them and seats each. They're placed in the middle of the view and selected, so you can drag them all into place |
| **1·2·3 Renumber** | Numbers the tables "Table 1, 2, 3…" left to right, top to bottom (also in the right-click menu) |
| **⤓ Image** | Downloads the plan as a PNG on white, with the client's name, to send to the family or the venue |

### 3D view
Press **3D** at the top right of the Layout or Seating tab to look round the room in 3D: tables with linens and centrepieces, gold chairs, the stage with its steps, the mandap's pillars and canopy, the dance floor and table numbers.

- **Overview**, **Top**, **From the entrance** and **From the stage** move the camera; drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom.
- **Walk** puts you at eye height at the entrance: drag to look around, **W A S D** or the arrow keys to walk.
- **Save image** downloads what you see, ready to send to the family.
- In the **Seating** tab the chairs are coloured by side, and hovering one shows who sits there.
- 3D is for looking round: switch back to **2D** to move things or seat guests. If a browser can't show 3D, it says so and the 2D plan works as usual.

With nothing selected, the **Room** panel sets the room's size and compares the seats in the plan with the guests coming and the venue's capacity. Items that overlap are outlined in red.

**Save as a venue plan** keeps the room for the next wedding at the same venue. **Start again** clears the plan (and its seating).

## Seating (Seating tab)
1. The list on the left shows the households still to seat for this function: those who said yes, with how many. Tick *Include guests who haven't replied* to seat ahead of replies.
2. **Drag** a household onto a table, or onto a particular seat; the table lights up as you pass over it. On a phone, **tap** the household, then tap a table. Hover a seat to see who sits there.
3. **Auto-seat** fills empty seats with everyone left, keeping households together and groups (Family, College…) at the same tables where it can.
4. Click a table to see who sits there, unseat someone or clear the table.
5. Seats are coloured by side: bride's, groom's or both.

**Needs attention** lists anyone seated who has since said no, households split over tables, and people who lost their seat when a table was made smaller or removed. **Remove people not coming** tidies the first kind in one go.

## The seating PDF
**Download seating PDF** makes an A4 document with:
1. the floor plan, with taken seats filled in
2. each table and who sits at it
3. a *Find your table* list, A–Z, for the welcome desk
