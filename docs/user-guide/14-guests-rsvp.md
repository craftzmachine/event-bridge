# Guests & RSVP

Keep each wedding's guest list, invite households to the right functions, and let guests answer from their phone.

Open **Guests & Seating** from the Dashboard. Every wedding is listed (drafts too) with its guests, how many are confirmed and its seating; press **Guests** on a wedding. The same tab is in each wedding's workspace under **Events**.

[Screenshot: Guests tab with reply cards]

## Adding guests
- **Add guest**: name on the invitation, phone/WhatsApp, how many people it's for, side (bride's, groom's, both), group (Family, College friends, Office…), and the functions they're invited to.
- **Paste a list**: copy rows from Excel or Google Sheets, or type one household per line: *name, phone, side, group, people*. Only the name is needed; a header row is skipped.

Everyone starts invited to every function. Open a guest to untick the ones they're not invited to.

## The RSVP link
1. In **RSVP link**, set a **reply-by** date, the **meal choices** (or leave blank), and an optional message.
2. Press **Create RSVP link**.
3. Each guest's **WhatsApp** button opens a message with their **personal link**. Nothing is sent until you press Send in WhatsApp.
4. The guest sees only their own invitation. For each function they choose **Joyfully accept**, **Not sure yet** or **Regretfully decline**, how many are coming, and their meal and dietary needs. They can change their answer until the reply-by date.
5. Replies come back when you open the Guests tab.

**Changed something?** After adding guests, changing functions or the date, press **Update RSVP link**. The links stay the same.

**Open link**: tick *Let people who aren't on the list reply* to get one link for a family WhatsApp group. People give their name and phone, and their replies arrive as new guests marked "added themselves".

**Turn off** stops the link working. Replies already in stay on the list.

## Reading the replies
- The cards at the top show, per function, how many people are **coming**, not coming, maybe, and how many households haven't replied.
- Filter the list by side or by reply.
- **Use confirmed headcount** sets the proposal's guest count to the most people coming to one function, so per-guest items (plates, chairs) follow it.
- You can record a reply yourself (from a phone call) by opening the guest.

## Privacy
- A personal link shows one household only, never the guest list.
- A wrong code, an ended link and a turned-off link all show the same message.
- Guests never appear on the proposal, PDF or Client Portal you share with the family.
- Duplicating a proposal doesn't copy its guests or seating.
