# Expenses

Record what's spent on each wedding and by the company, with proof, staff advances, and handovers that a colleague reviews.

[Screenshot: Expenses home]

## The Expenses home
- This month's spending.
- The 8 most recent expenses across every ledger.
- Every **ledger**: **Company** first, then each event. Search to find one.
- **Receive a handover** (for reviewers).

## Recording an expense
1. Press **Record expense** on the Dashboard, or **Add expense** on the Expenses page or in a ledger.
2. Choose what it's **For**: Company, or an event. It starts on the ledger you used last.
3. Attach proof: **Take photo**, **Choose screenshot**, or paste a GPay screenshot.
4. Fill in the **amount**, **date**, **who was paid** (suggestions come from your vendor list), **category**, **who paid** (you, the company, or a staff advance) and the **UPI transaction ID**.
5. **More details** has the time and the payee's UPI ID.
6. **Save.**

### Checks while saving
- A **UPI transaction ID already recorded** for that ledger **blocks** the save — nothing is entered twice.
- You're **warned** about: the same screenshot used before, the same amount + date + payee, no proof, a large amount, or a date outside the event's dates.

### Nothing is deleted outright
Removing an expense needs a reason and can be undone with **Restore**. Every edit is kept in the expense's **History**.

## The company ledger
Company expenses are kept **by month**. Pick the month at the top; its handover covers that month only.

## Staff advances
Give a staff member money to spend on site:
1. In a ledger, **Give money** → who it's for, the amount, a settle-by date and an optional spending limit.
2. Their expenses can then be paid **from the advance**. The card shows what's left.
3. Record **top-ups**, **returns** and **reimbursements** as they happen. Any movement can be reversed with a reason.
4. **Settlement** shows what's owed back (or owed to the staff member). Accepted expenses need proof or a reviewer's check; queried ones are held.
5. **Close** the advance when the balance is zero, or write off the difference with a reason.

Your admin can limit who may manage advances.

## Handover and review
When the event is done (or the month ends for Company):
1. **Prepare handover** checks for missing proof and duplicates, then creates a **.zip** with the expenses, an Excel ledger, a PDF summary and every proof image.
2. Send the file to the colleague who'll check it.
3. They open **Expenses → Receive a handover**, mark each expense **Checked**, **Query** or **Reject**, and **Sign off**. This creates a reply file.
4. **Import the reply** on your device. The reviews and advance settlements are recorded on each expense.
5. Once reconciled, **Clear from this device** removes the event's expenses (open advances are kept).

The reviewer must be someone else: anyone who recorded or edited the expenses, or gave, held or moved advance money, can't review.

## Tips
- Use your own phone; expenses and screenshots stay in that browser.
- On phones, the category, payer and flag filters fold behind **Filters**.

## Common questions
**Can it read my Google Pay payments automatically?** Not yet. Entry is manual; reading screenshots automatically is on the roadmap.
