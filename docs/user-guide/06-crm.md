# CRM

Track enquiries from the first message to a booking, and see every client behind your proposals.

[Screenshot: CRM board]

## Adding an enquiry
Press **New enquiry** and fill in: name, mobile, where it came from (Instagram, referral, venue…), the occasion, date, guests, budget, the venue in mind and notes.

## Working an enquiry
1. Move it through the stages: **New → Contacted → Meeting → Proposal sent → Won** or **Lost** (with a reason).
2. Set the **next follow-up** date.
3. **Log** calls, meetings, WhatsApp messages and notes as you go. The activity log keeps the history.
4. **Create proposal** opens a new proposal filled in from the enquiry. From then on the enquiry follows the proposal: sent, accepted (Won) or lost.

## Views
- **Board** — a column per stage.
- **List** — follow-ups due first.
- **Clients** — everyone behind your proposals, one per mobile number, with their proposals.

## Tips
- Follow-ups due appear on the Dashboard under **Needs attention** and on the CRM tile.
- Use **Export** / **Import** to move the CRM to another device.
