# Getting started

## Signing in
1. Open your company's Event Bridge address and choose **Team sign in**.
2. Enter your work email and password. Email isn't case-sensitive; the password is.
3. You land on the **Dashboard**.

- A session lasts **12 hours** on a device, or until you press **Sign out**.
- After 5 wrong passwords, sign-in pauses for a short while. Ask your admin if you've forgotten your password.
- Opened a link to a page while signed out? After signing in you're taken straight back to it.

## Finding your way around
There are no menus. The **Dashboard is the hub**:
- The **apps bar** at the top of the Dashboard opens every app.
- Every page's breadcrumb starts with **← Dashboard** to come back.
- The **top bar** on every page has:
  - the **crest** — back to the Dashboard
  - the **theme** button — Dark, Parinay, Solarized Light, Solarized Dark, Nord, White
  - **Aa** — cycles the font (Classic, Sans, Mono)
  - **Help** — the in-app guide, searchable
  - your email and **Sign out**

Both theme and font are remembered on this device.

## Where your work is saved
Your proposals, enquiries, tasks, vendors and questionnaires are saved **in this browser, on this device**, automatically as you work. Expenses and their screenshots are saved in the browser too.

That means:
- Work on one device per person, and **back up regularly**.
- **Clearing the browser's site data deletes this device's work.** Don't use private/incognito windows for real work.

### Backing up and moving to another device
1. Open **Proposal Studio**.
2. Press **Back up all**. A file downloads with every proposal.
3. On the other device, press **Import** and choose that file.

The CRM, Tasks, Vendors and Questionnaires each have their own **Export** and **Import**. A reminder appears on the Studio home if you haven't backed up for 7 days.

> Cloud sync across devices is on the roadmap. Until then, backups are the safety net.
