# Wedding Quest

A light-hearted guide for new team members: a spotlight tour and missions that tick themselves off as you work.

1. Press the **gold button** in the bottom-right corner of any page to open the quest log.
2. **Take the tour** — a two-minute walk through the Dashboard, Proposal Studio, the CRM and Events. Use **Next** and **Back**, the arrow keys, or **Skip tour**.
3. **Missions** such as *Start your first proposal* tick themselves off when you do them. **Show me** takes you to the right page.
4. Each mission earns **XP**, taking you from *Fresh Garland* to *Muhurtham Legend*.

Progress is saved on this device. A welcome card appears on the Dashboard on your first visit.
