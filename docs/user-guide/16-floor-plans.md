# Floor Plans

Floor plans and seating that **aren't tied to a wedding**: a venue's standard layout, a mock-up to show a family at the first meeting, or a plan to practise on. They use the same editor as a wedding's Layout and Seating tabs, including 3D.

Open **Floor Plans** from the Dashboard.

[Screenshot: Floor Plans list]

## Making a plan
1. Press **New plan**: give it a name, the venue (suggestions come from the Venue Directory) and roughly how many guests.
2. **Layout:** start from *Reception rounds*, *Banquet long tables* or *Mandap with chair rows* (sized for your guests), a plan saved for this venue, or an empty room. Then add, move and size things as described in [Venue layout & seating](15-layout-seating.md).
3. **Guests:** add households one by one, or paste a list from Excel or Google Sheets (name, phone, side, group, people). There's no RSVP here: everyone on the list is seated.
4. **Seating:** drag households onto tables, or press **Auto-seat**.

**Plan details** (at the top of a plan) holds the name, venue, guests expected and notes.

## Professional tools
Everything in the [Layout editor](15-layout-seating.md) works here too, including:
- **⛶ Fullscreen** (or F) for the whole screen,
- **🗺 Background** to trace the venue's own drawing at the right scale,
- **☰ Layers** to find, hide, lock and reorder items,
- **✓ Checks** for aisles, gaps between tables, the stage and entrances,
- **🖨 Print** to scale (1:50 to 1:500 on A4 or A3) with a legend.

## Counting and ordering
- The **Counts** panel totals tables (by size and seats), chairs, chair rows and the mandap, stage, aisle and other items, as you work.
- **Inventory ▾** downloads the list for the rental vendor as **PDF** or **Excel**: chairs, tables by size, tablecloths to the floor, runners, centrepieces and décor with their sizes.
- **Tables ▾** sets every round table (or long table, or every table and row) to the same number of seats, or spreads a number of guests across them.

## Using a plan for a wedding
- **Copy into a wedding** (top of a plan): pick the wedding and the function. Only the layout is copied; the wedding seats its own guest list. If the function already has a plan, you're asked before it's replaced.
- The other way round, a wedding's Layout tab has **Save as a floor plan**, which keeps a copy of its layout here without the family's guests.

## The tutorial
**Start the tutorial** (top of Floor Plans) opens a small practice plan and walks you through the editor in 11 short steps:

1. Add a round table
2. Move it
3. Resize it
4. Change the chairs
5. Lay an aisle
6. Size the mandap
7. Select several
8. Chairs for every table
9. Count and order
10. Undo
11. Look round in 3D

Each step tells you what to do, rings the button or area to use, and **ticks off by itself** when you've done it. **Back** and **Skip this step** are there if you need them. Restart it any time with **🎓 Tutorial** on any Layout tab.

## Keeping plans
Plans are saved on this device. **Export** downloads them all as a file; **Import** on another device brings them in. **Duplicate** makes a copy to try another idea; **Delete** removes a plan for good.
