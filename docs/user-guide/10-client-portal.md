# Client Portal

A private, password-protected page for each family with their proposal, their to-dos, the payment schedule and their questionnaires.

[Screenshot: client portal as the family sees it]

## Creating a portal
1. Open **Client Portal** and pick a wedding.
2. **Create portal** gives you a link and a password.
3. **Send them separately** — for example the link by email and the password by WhatsApp.

## What the family can do
- Read the proposal and **approve** it or **ask for changes** with a comment.
- Tick off **their** checklist tasks.
- See the **payment schedule** and what's been received.
- Answer **questionnaires**.

## Keeping it current
- **Update portal** sends the latest proposal, tasks and payments on the **same link and password**. Their decision and answers are kept.
- **Turn off** ends access immediately.

## What the family never sees
Your costs and margin, their budget figure, the team's own tasks, the coordination sheet and internal notes.

## Common questions
**The family says the link doesn't work.** A wrong password looks the same as an ended link on purpose. Check the password, or turn the portal off and create a new one.
