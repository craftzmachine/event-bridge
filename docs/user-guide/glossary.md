# Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Element** | One item from your catalogue (a stage, a photographer, a meal) added to a proposal. |
| **Category** | A section of the proposal (Décor, Catering…), in the order your admin sets. |
| **Function** | One part of the celebration (Haldi, Sangeet, Reception), with its own date and subtotal. |
| **As per actuals** | An item with no fixed rate, charged at what it actually costs. Listed, but adds nothing to the Sub Total. |
| **Upgrade** | An extra on an element. *No* = not offered, *Offer* = listed under Additional Options, *Include* = added to the price. |
| **Additional Options** | Items and upgrades offered for the family to choose. **Never** in the Grand Total. |
| **Sub Total** | The sum of included items: qty × rate plus included upgrades. |
| **Co-ordination charges** | Sub Total × your co-ordination % (set per proposal; default from your company settings). |
| **Rounded Off** | The difference when you round the Grand Total to a neater figure. |
| **Grand Total** | Sub Total + co-ordination + round-off. Before GST. |
| **GST** | Shown as a reference figure on top of the Grand Total; payment milestones use the total including GST. |
| **Quotation reference** | The proposal's unique code, `YYYYDDMM-ABC` (year, day, month, 3 letters). Can't be edited. |
| **Package** | A saved starting point for a proposal. |
| **Cost / margin** | What an item costs you per unit, and the difference from the price. Internal only. |
| **Milestone** | One payment in the schedule (for example 70% on booking, 30% a week before the first event). |
| **Ledger** | The list of expenses for one event, or for the company by month. |
| **Advance** | Money given to a staff member to spend; their expenses come out of it. |
| **Settlement** | What a staff member owes back, or is owed, once their expenses are reviewed. |
| **Handover** | A .zip of a ledger's expenses and proofs, sent to a colleague to review and sign off. |
| **Client Portal** | A family's private page with their proposal, to-dos, payments and questionnaires. |
| **Share** | A private, password-protected link to a proposal (web page, PDF or Excel). |
