# Events and the wedding workspace

Every wedding in play, each with its own workspace for the checklist, coordination sheet, questionnaires, payments and expenses.

[Screenshot: Events list]

## The events list
- Shows **accepted** proposals and anything with an event date. Lost proposals appear only under **All**.
- Tabs: **Upcoming** (soonest first), **Past** (most recent first), **All**.
- Search by client. Each row shows a countdown ("In 12 days", "On now", "3 days ago") and checklist progress.

## The workspace
Open a wedding to see its tabs. Each tab has its own link, so you can send a colleague straight to it.

### Overview
Client, dates, venue, guests and functions at a glance; a **WhatsApp** button for the client; a warning when the guests exceed the venue's capacity.

### Guests, Layout and Seating
The guest list and RSVP link, the floor plan for each function, and who sits where. See [Guests & RSVP](14-guests-rsvp.md) and [Venue layout & seating](15-layout-seating.md).

### Checklist
1. Press **Start the checklist**. It fills from your team's checklist template.
2. Each task is due a set number of days before the **first event date** (or on a fixed date), and belongs to the **team** or the **family**.
3. Tick tasks off, or use the **Board** to move them between **To do**, **Doing** and **Done**. Done records who and when.
4. Tasks are grouped into **Overdue**, **This week**, **Later**, **No date** and **Done**.

Family tasks appear in their [Client Portal](10-client-portal.md); team tasks never do.

### Coordination
The **On-Site Event Coordination & Responsibility Sheet**, one per function:
- contacts, team on duty, hostess stations
- timeline and cues
- duties for the kind of function (Hindu ceremony, reception, sangeet, other — picked from the function's name; change it if needed)
- readiness checks, escalation, issue log and handover

Press **Export PDF** to print it for the on-site team. Blank fields print as empty ruled boxes to fill in by hand.

### Questionnaires
The questionnaires sent to this wedding and the family's answers. You can also type answers in from a phone call. See [Questionnaires](09-questionnaires.md).

### Payments
The payment schedule and every payment received. Record a payment with its amount, date and method, then **download a receipt** (numbered `R-<reference>-<n>`). Overdue and upcoming milestones show on the Dashboard.

### Expenses
This wedding's expense ledger. See [Expenses](05-expenses.md).

### Proposal
The proposal as the client sees it, with a link to edit it in Proposal Studio.

## Tips
- The checklist and coordination sheet are internal: they never appear on anything the client sees.
- Duplicating a proposal does **not** copy its checklist, coordination sheet or answers.
