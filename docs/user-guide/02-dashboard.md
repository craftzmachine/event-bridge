# The Dashboard

The Dashboard is where you start every day. It answers three questions: what's coming up, what needs me, and how is the business doing.

[Screenshot: Dashboard]

## What's on it
1. **Greeting and quick actions** — **New proposal** and **Record expense**.
2. **Apps bar** — one button per app.
3. **Four figures:**
   - **Upcoming events** — weddings with a date ahead
   - **Awaiting decision** — proposals sent but not yet accepted or lost
   - **Received this month** — payments recorded this month
   - **Needs attention** — how many items are waiting below
4. **Upcoming events** as a **list** or a **month calendar** (switch with the toggle; use the arrows to move between months). Click a client to open their wedding workspace.
5. **Needs attention**, one list in priority order:
   - overdue payments
   - overdue checklist and team tasks
   - proposals that need a follow-up
   - enquiry follow-ups due (from the CRM)
   - payments due soon
   - questionnaires the family hasn't answered
6. **Pipeline** — one bar showing draft → sent → accepted, with counts and values. Lost proposals are left out.
7. **What's selling** — the elements that appear most in your proposals.
8. **This month's spending** — with **Add expense**.

## Tips
- Work down **Needs attention** each morning; each row links to where you fix it.
- **Record expense** remembers the last ledger you used, so on-site entries take seconds.
- Proposals shared with clients also show whether the family has viewed, approved or asked for changes.
