# Vendors

The rolodex: who to call, what they charge and what you've paid them.

## Adding a vendor
**Add vendor** → category, contact person, phone, UPI ID, area, prices, a 1–5 rating, tags and notes.

## Finding a vendor
Search by name or tag, or filter by **category** and **area**. Each card has **WhatsApp** and **Call** buttons.

## Spend per vendor
Each card shows what's been paid to that vendor across events, taken from your recorded expenses (matched by name).

## Team list
Your admin can publish a read-only team vendor list that everyone sees alongside their own.

## Tips
- When you record an expense, the payee field suggests names from the rolodex — use the same spelling so spend adds up.
- **Export** / **Import** moves the list to another device.
