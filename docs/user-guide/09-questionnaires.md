# Questionnaires

Build a set of questions once, send it to a wedding, and keep the family's answers.

## Creating a questionnaire
1. **New questionnaire**, or start from the team's planning questionnaire.
2. Add questions. Types: short answer, paragraph, one choice, several choices, date, number, yes/no.
3. Mark questions **required** and add **hints**.

## Sending it
Under **Send to a wedding**, pick the event. A copy goes onto that wedding, so editing the template later doesn't change what was sent.

## Getting answers
- The family answers in their [Client Portal](10-client-portal.md).
- Or fill answers in yourself from a phone call, in the wedding's **Questionnaires** tab.
- Answers from the portal come back when you open the Portal page or the wedding.

## Tips
- Unanswered questionnaires show on the Dashboard under **Needs attention**.
- Answers are never copied when a proposal is duplicated, and never appear on a shared proposal.
